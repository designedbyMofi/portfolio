# Portfolio and job-search audit — 7 September 2026

## Verdict

The portfolio shows unusually strong visual craft and interaction polish. Mofifoluwa should be positioned for Senior Product Designer, hands-on Product Design Lead, or Founding Product Designer roles in fintech, AI, insurance, and complex B2B SaaS.

The main hiring risk is not quality. It is proof density and consistency. The default Shots page emphasizes interface craft, while the Projects page has only one available case study. The resume and public profiles also contain stale or conflicting information that can weaken trust.

## Captured steps

1. `01-home.png` — Home/Shots. Strong visual impression and clear domain positioning, but the default experience signals UI craft before product outcomes.
2. `02-projects.png` — Projects. The Vurt case study is promising, but one live study and one “coming soon” item are too little evidence for senior applications.
3. `03-vurt-case-study.png` — Vurt. Strong ownership, metrics, systems thinking, and a credible marketplace/trust narrative. It needs sharper evidence for research, trade-offs, and shipped outcomes.
4. `04-resume.png` — On-site resume. Strong experience and several excellent metrics, but overlapping roles need employment-type labels and dense paragraphs should become outcome-led bullets.
5. `cv/page-1.png` and `cv/page-2.png` — Downloadable resume. Page two has a visibly clipped right column, and extracted text splits words such as “Strategy” and “Figma,” creating ATS risk.

## Highest-impact changes

1. Make Projects the default hiring path, or place three case-study cards before the visual shot gallery.
2. Add Cyvi and PressOne/Juliet case studies. These are the strongest complements to Vurt because they show enterprise complexity, AI, and measurable outcomes.
3. Rebuild the downloadable resume as a clean one-page, single-column document.
4. Explain overlapping engagements consistently as full-time, contract, fractional, or advisory work.
5. Update LinkedIn, Behance, and Medium so title, dates, company names, email, location, and positioning match the portfolio.
6. Use the existing audience to publish decision-led case-study excerpts instead of generic design inspiration.

## Accessibility and usability risks visible in screenshots

- The floating bottom navigation overlaps page content at the audited viewport.
- Several secondary labels use small, low-contrast gray text.
- The portfolio has reasonable semantic structure and keyboard-oriented controls in the captured DOM, but screenshots cannot establish contrast ratios, focus visibility, screen-reader quality, or complete keyboard behavior.

## Evidence limits

The audit covered the live desktop portfolio at its default browser viewport, the downloadable PDF, and search-visible public profile information. It did not verify authenticated LinkedIn, X, or Instagram content, mobile behavior, analytics, or the provenance of the stated performance metrics.
